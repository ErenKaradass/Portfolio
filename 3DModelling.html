My 3D Modeling Work

Here is a collection of my 3D modeling projects. I have worked with various software tools such as Blender, Fusion360, Maya3D and Zbrush. To create detailed models for games and other applications.

and for texturing I use 3DSubstance Painter or i get textures from other sources such as from the internet.


A lot of my 3D modeling work focuses on creating assets for game development. I enjoy the process of sculpting characters and environments from scratch, using my favorite tool like Blender.

My approach to 3D modeling is centered around creating high-quality, optimized models that can be used in various applications, from video games to architectural visualizations.

Some are for rendering some are for 3D printing and others for game engines.

Most of my work is done in Blender, but I am also familiar with other software like Fusion360 and Maya3D.

I use Blender for Rendering and 3D Modelling for games, fusion360 for more complex modeling tasks, and Maya3D for animation and simulation.
